Join us for our next Ruby::AZ meetup at **CO+HOOTS in Mesa**! This month, we have an exciting double feature exploring two very different, highly entertaining topics: AI-assisted video game development in Ruby, and leveraging LLMs and MCP for automated web scraping.

## Talks

### Developing a Game in Ruby &mdash; Jay McGavren

Jay McGavren, author of O'Reilly's *Head First Ruby*, joins us to share the story of creating [*Super Legally Distinct Pipefitting Brothers*](https://jay.mcgavren.com/super-legally-distinct-pipefitting-bros/)&mdash;an AI-assisted game written in Ruby and compiled to WebAssembly via [Wasmcart](https://wasmcart.org/languages/ruby) (connecting back to Luis Montes' WASM game engine talk from our July meetup). Jay will walk through the fun, surprises, and developer workflow of building games with Ruby and AI.

<img src="/images/super_legally_distinct.png" alt="Super Legally Distinct Pipefitting Brothers" style="display: block; margin: 1.5rem auto; border-radius: 12px; max-width: 100%;">

### Web Scraping via LLMs & MCP &mdash; Chris Irish

Chris Irish will share a real-world look at using LLMs and the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to scrape Upwork and identify high-value jobs. He'll touch on the practicalities of disposable, AI-generated scraper code, MCP integrations, and what happens when you let models handle the extraction dirty work.

We're looking forward to seeing you at [CO+HOOTS, Mesa](https://cohoots.com/locations/mesa/) on Thursday, November 19th at 7:00 PM. Come hang out, learn something new, and catch up with your fellow Arizona Rubyists!
