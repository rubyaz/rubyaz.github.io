This month we teamed up with our friends at [**GDG Phoenix / PHX Android**](https://www.meetup.com/phx-android/) for a hands-on build night at [HeatSync Labs](https://heatsynclabs.org).

Rather than a passive lecture, this was an open working workshop and hack night focused on what developers can build with AI without spending a dime -- running models locally, exploring generous free tiers, and sharing practical tooling across the Phoenix tech community.

## Talks

### Ruby AI Gems: Building AI Applications in Ruby &mdash; Marc Heiligers

Marc gave an overview of the evolving AI ecosystem in Ruby, showing how developers can build production-ready AI features, RAG pipelines, and context-aware agents natively in Ruby without vendor lock-in.

* **[RubyLLM](https://rubyllm.com/)**: A unified interface across 17+ LLM providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, etc.) offering clean Ruby ergonomics, streaming responses, and structured JSON outputs out of the box.
* **[Ruby MCP Client](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/docs/2026-07-28/develop/build-client#ruby)**: Standardizing context integration via the Model Context Protocol, connecting AI models directly and securely to local files, tools, and databases.
* **Andrew Kane's ML Gems**: Bringing native machine learning directly to Ruby with Torch.rb (deep learning & neural networks), Informers (native transformer models for embeddings and sentiment analysis), and Pgvector-ruby (vector similarity search for RAG in Active Record).
