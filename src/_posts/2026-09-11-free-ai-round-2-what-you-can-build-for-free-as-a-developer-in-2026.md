This month we're teaming up with our friends at [**GDG Phoenix / PHX Android**](https://www.meetup.com/phx-android/) for a hands-on build night at [HeatSync Labs](https://heatsynclabs.org).

Instead of a passive slide lecture, this is an open working workshop focused on what you can build with AI without spending a dime -- no monthly subscriptions, no burning through paid API credits, and no surprise cloud bills.

Whether you want to wire up a local model (via Ollama or LM Studio) to a Ruby script or Rails app, experiment with zero-cost hosting, or just need an excuse to knock out that stubborn side project you've been putting off, all stacks and skill levels are welcome.

## What We'll Be Doing

* **Local AI on your machine:** Running models locally for $0 without external API dependencies.
* **Zero-cost hosting:** Getting projects live using generous free tiers (Cloudflare, GitHub Pages, Google Cloud).
* **Open build & hack session:** Share an idea, roll up your sleeves, and build alongside folks across the Phoenix tech community.

Bring your laptop, a charger, and an idea you'd like to hack on.

See you Thursday, September 17th at 7:00 PM at [HeatSync Labs](https://heatsynclabs.org)!
